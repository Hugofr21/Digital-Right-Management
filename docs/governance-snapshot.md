## Overview

This document provides a snapshot of the project's governance model, decision-making structure, and current maintainership status. It is intended to provide contributors, stakeholders, and auditors with a concise overview of how the project is managed.

## Governance Model

The project follows a DRM governance model.

Key principles:

* Transparency in decision-making.
* Open participation for contributors.
* Documented review and approval processes.
* Accountability of maintainers and project leaders.

## Decision-Making Process

Major decisions are made through:

1. Public discussion in issues and pull requests.
2. Technical evaluation of proposed changes.
3. Review by the Lead Maintainer.
4. Final approval by the Lead Maintainer when consensus cannot be reached.
